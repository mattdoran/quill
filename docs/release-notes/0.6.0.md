## Quill 0.6.0

**Remember Voice**
- Click **Remember Voice** to save a speaker's name and voice on this Mac.
- In later meetings, if an unnamed voice sounds like someone you saved, such as Alice, click **Use Alice** to fill in the name.
- **Forget Remembered Voices…** removes them all. Names already in transcripts stay.

**Other changes**
- **Separate Voices…** now separates Local and Remote voices together. Under "How many people spoke?", change either or both.
- **Copy Markdown** (Shift-Command-C) copies the whole transcript with speaker names and times.
