## Quill 0.8.0

**Voice separation**
- The number of people is now an estimate. Quill may find an extra voice rather than mixing two people together.
- Give two voices the same name and Quill treats them as one person. **Not the Same Person** separates them again.
- Voices that are clearly the same person are combined automatically.

**Transcript review**
- Opening a second transcript no longer stops the first window from saving names or closing.

**Call detection**
- The Detected prompt stays until you record, dismiss it or the call ends.
- Quill recognises more call apps, including Zoom Phone, Webex, Aircall, 8x8 Work and Google Meet web apps.
