## Quill 0.9.0

**Transcripts**
- Each person's turn now sits under one name and time; short replies show within it as "[Matt: yeah]".
- In the transcript window, click a time to play from there. Click again to stop.
- When a call plays through your Mac's speakers, fewer of its words reappear as yours.
- Transcript files now start with the meeting's date, time, length and people. Existing files are updated, except any you've edited.

**Other changes**
- "Meetings…" lists every meeting, replacing "Open Last Transcript", "Review Earlier Transcript…" and "Open Recordings Folder".
- Let AI assistants on this Mac, such as Claude Code, read your meetings: in Settings, under "Other apps", click **Install**.
