## Quill 0.5.0

**Meeting companion**
- Recordings started from "Meeting detected" now stop 20 seconds after the meeting ends, unless you click **Keep Recording**.
- If the companion isn't showing, you'll get a "Meeting ended?" notification.
- Remembers where you drag it, even after restarting Quill.

**Other changes**
- While a Quill window is open, Quill shows in the Dock and Command-Tab.
- To bring it back, choose **Show Quill Window** from the menu bar icon.
